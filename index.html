Limits of these records

  • The poison is not named.No table says whether a bait carried 1080 or PAPP. The ACT Government's public page on dingoes and wild dogs lists 1080 baiting as its poison method.
  • One program only.These are wild dog and dingo records. Fox, rabbit and pig baiting, and Canberra's urban reserves, are not in this release.
  • Private land is blank.
  • Status is a snapshot.
  • Early checks have no dates.
  • Not a safety map.Do not use this to decide where a dog is safe. Baiting changes between seasons. Follow the warning signs on site.
